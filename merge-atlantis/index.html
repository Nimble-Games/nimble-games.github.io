What we are testing

The bet behind Merge Atlantis

Hypothesis

Players stay longer when nothing they make is taken away, and every merge ends up in a home they decorate.

  • 1Goals, not orders. Goals count what you do. They never take an item away.
  • 2What you build stays. Finished decorations go to your aquarium, and placing them opens the next area.
  • 3Creatures do the work. Call a jellyfish or an octopus to harvest and build, and collect pets.

It works if players keep decorating and coming back for longer than in today's order-based merge games.

  • One-screen merge

    Order-based merge

    Easy merge with a story. What you make is handed over in orders and is gone.

  • Big-map merge

    Big-map merge

    What you merge stays on the map, but the map is the game: wide to pan and search.

  • Decoration meta

    Aquarium decorating

    A loved meta: decorate a tank, fill it, open the next. Fed by a puzzle that is not merge.

  • Our first bet

    Merge Crown

    Orders pay for a kingdom that stays, with creatures from island adventures.

  • This bet

    Merge Atlantis

    One merge board, goals instead of orders, and everything you make decorates an underwater kingdom.

Categories describe the genre in general, not any single title. Placement is our qualitative view.

The one big change

Goals instead of orders

In most merge games the best thing you make leaves the board for good. Here it never does.

Step 1

    Your aquarium

    Place it. Fill it. Open the next one.

    An underwater kingdom you decorate piece by piece, and keep coming back to.

    • Place anywhere

      Free placement on the sand. Pieces further back draw smaller, like a real scene.

    • Fill the meter

      Every decoration adds deco points. A full meter completes the area.

    • Open the next area

      A finished area opens the next one and hands out its welcome gift.

    • Revisit and redecorate

      Every area stays open. Move, mirror or put back anything, any time.

    Four areas, one wonder each

    1. 1

      Sunlit Shallows

      Kelp, pearls, shell cottages and tide fountains. Wonder: Great Conch Conservatory

    2. 2

      Coral Gardens

      Fan coral, sea blossoms, reef nurseries and seahorse havens. Wonder: Living Coral Cathedral

    3. 3

      Royal Atlantis

      Crystal gardens, relics, mosaic villas and tide engines. Wonder: Heart of Atlantis

    4. 4

      Shipwreck Cove

      Wreck salvage, treasure, mariners' relics and hideaways. Wonder: Crown's Rest Galleon

    In the prototype: all four areas, with 56 decorations painted across 7 collections (more planned). Designed and painted, coming next: 16 merge chains of seven tiers and four wonders built in stages.

    The creature layer

    Sea creatures and pets

    Creatures do the work on the reef, in 3D with a soft jelly look. The ones you earn come home to your aquarium.

    1. Call

      Drag a creature onto a job, or let the nearest one take it.

    2. Work

      The jellyfish works in glowing bubbles, the octopus with its arms.

    3. Heal the reef

      Burst water orbs from the well to bring dead reef back to life.

    4. Earn pets

      Finish an area to earn a pet. The shark is the first.

    Why players come back

    Energy refills

    Merging uses energy. It refills over time, so players return when it is full.

    One more decoration

    Every piece moves the meter toward the next area. One more decoration is a reason to come back.

    Creatures keep working

    Harvests and builds run on timers, so there is always something ready.
