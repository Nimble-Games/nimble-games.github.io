Step 1

Merge

Each board is a grid of items. Tap a producer to make new items, then drag two matching items together to create the next item in the chain.

  • Every board makes its own kind of goods, like fruit on the Orchard.
  • Merging uses energy, which refills over time.
  • New boards open as the town builds workshops.

Your kingdom

Expand it. Build it. Make it yours.

A town you shape with your own hands, and love to look at.

  • Expand

    Clear wild land and push your borders outward.

The adventure layer

Islands and magical creatures

Every island is an adventure, and every creature you find comes home with you.

  1. Travel

    Pick a new island on the world map.

  2. Heal the land

    Merge to bring the island back to life.

  3. Hatch creatures

    Merge eggs to discover dragons and unicorns.

  4. See them at home

    Your creatures live in your kingdom, and fill the Breed Book.

Why players come back

Energy refills

Merging uses energy. It refills over time, so players return when it is full.

Gems skip the wait

Gems buy energy and speed things up. This is where players spend money.

Always something cooking

Buildings and workshops keep working while players are away.
